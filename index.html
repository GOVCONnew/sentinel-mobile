      Help · v2.0

      What SENTINEL can and cannot do

      Foreground only. iOS Safari suspends camera, microphone and scripts when the screen locks, another app opens or the tab is hidden. SENTINEL cannot monitor in the background. Every such interruption is written to the log as a MONITORING GAP with its duration.

      Everything runs on this phone. Frames, audio and events are never uploaded. Models are downloaded once and cached.

      1 · HTTPS

      Camera and microphone need HTTPS. Use https://govconnew.github.io/sentinel-mobile/.

      2 · Permissions

      Tap START, then Allow camera and microphone. Denied by mistake? Tap aA in the address bar → Website Settings → Camera / Microphone → Allow, then reload. Or Settings › Apps › Safari › Camera / Microphone (older iOS: Settings › Safari). iOS may ask again after a reload or when launched from the Home Screen. Location: Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services › Safari Websites.

      3 · Keep it running

      Add to Home Screen for fullscreen. Set Auto-Lock › Never and keep the phone on power.

      4 · Storage

      History and snapshots live in this browser's storage. Safari may delete website data after ~7 days without use (less likely for Home-Screen apps), and when the device is low on space. Export regularly. SENTINEL requests persistent storage; the History tab shows usage and whether it was granted.

      5 · How detections become alerts

      Raw model outputs (dashed, dim) → confirmed tracks (score above the category threshold in N of M frames) → events (confirmed track inside ROI / outside exclusion zones) → incidents (events grouped in time). Sound and visual evidence in the same time window are marked “co-occurring — possibly related”; SENTINEL never claims they come from the same source.

      6 · Motion

      Motion uses camera-shake compensation, an adaptive background that learns swaying foliage, illumination normalisation and blob persistence/direction checks. By default motion only makes the detector run faster; enable “Coherent motion alone may open an incident” to alert on motion.

      7 · Limits

      COCO classes only — no deer/fox/boar classes; wild animals appear as the nearest class (dog, horse, cow, sheep, bear…). Small or distant targets, dense occlusion and night scenes reduce accuracy. YAMNet confuses wind/rain with vehicles; v2 suppresses this but also lowers sensitivity in heavy rain.
